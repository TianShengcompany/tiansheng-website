Our Story

From a Workshop
to a Global Enterprise

TianSheng was founded in 1998 by a team of aluminum engineers who shared a singular vision: to create building envelope systems that match the world's finest �?at a price that makes them accessible to every market.

Starting with a single extrusion line in a 2,000 m² workshop, we have grown into a 50,000 m² manufacturing campus with 12 extrusion lines, fully automated powder coating and anodizing plants, and a dedicated research & development center staffed by 40+ engineers.

Today, TianSheng exports to over 30 countries across Africa, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and the Americas. Our systems are installed in luxury villas, commercial towers, government buildings, and hospitality projects �?a testament to the trust our partners place in us.
